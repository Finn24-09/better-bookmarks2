# 🌟 Code of Conduct

Better Bookmarks 2 is an open, friendly place to build software. This page describes the kind of community we want and how to flag things that fall short of it.

---

## 🤝 Our Values

We want this project to be a place where anyone can contribute and ask questions — regardless of experience, background, or how they came to be here. The behaviours we expect from everyone in our spaces:

- 💬 **Be respectful.** Treat others the way you would want to be treated. Disagreement is fine; disrespect is not.
- 🧠 **Assume good faith.** Most problems are misunderstandings. Ask before accusing.
- 🛠️ **Keep feedback constructive.** Critique the code or the idea, not the person behind it. Offer a path forward when you point out a problem.
- 🌱 **Make room for beginners.** Nobody is born knowing how this stuff works. A patient answer today builds a contributor for tomorrow.
- 🎯 **Stay on topic.** Issues, pull requests, and discussions are for the project. Keep the conversation focused on the work in front of us.
- 🪪 **Respect other people's privacy.** Do not share someone else's information without their permission. Redact identifiers from logs and screenshots before posting them.

---

## 🚫 What Is Not Welcome

Behaviour that makes other contributors feel unsafe, unwelcome, or unable to participate. In particular:

- 🛑 Personal attacks, insults, or sustained hostility toward any individual.
- 🛑 Targeted unwanted attention or repeated contact after being asked to stop.
- 🛑 Threats of any kind.
- 🛑 Sharing another person's private information without their explicit consent.
- 🛑 Any conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional collaboration.

The maintainers ultimately decide whether a given behaviour falls in this category. We will use judgement, not a rulebook.

---

## 🌍 Where This Applies

This code of conduct applies everywhere the project lives:

- 📦 This GitHub repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, and commit messages.
- 💬 Any future official channel the project may add (chat, mailing list, etc.).
- 🗣️ Any space where someone is officially representing the project — using a project account, speaking on the project's behalf at an event, or similar.

It applies to everyone who participates: maintainers, contributors, reviewers, issue reporters, and casual visitors.

---

## 📬 Reporting a Concern

If you see or experience something that you believe violates this code, please report it privately. **The only private channel this repository has is GitHub's Private Vulnerability Reporting form.** It is named for security reports but works equally well for any sensitive private communication to the maintainers:

👉 **[Open a private report](https://github.com/Finn24-09/better-bookmarks2/security/advisories/new)**

When you open the report:

- Use a subject line like `[Conduct] <short description>` so it can be triaged correctly.
- Include what happened, who was involved, where it happened (links to the relevant issue, PR, or commit), and any context you think matters.
- Tell us what outcome you are looking for, if you have one in mind.

You can also contact a maintainer directly via their GitHub profile if you prefer. Anonymous reports are accepted but harder to follow up on.

### ⏰ What to Expect

- 📨 Acknowledgement within **2 business days**.
- 🔒 Confidential review. We will not share your identity or the contents of your report with anyone outside the review process without your explicit permission.
- ⚖️ A timely, proportionate response. We will tell you what we decided and why.

---

## 🛡️ Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they judge appropriate, scaled to the impact of the behaviour. From least to most serious, this generally looks like:

- 📝 **Private clarification** — a quiet conversation about the impact of the behaviour and what should change.
- ⚠️ **Public warning** — a maintainer comment on the relevant thread explaining the issue.
- 🚷 **Temporary restriction** — the person is asked to step back from project spaces for a defined period.
- 🛑 **Permanent ban** — the person is removed from project spaces and blocked from re-entering them.

Maintainers will document each enforcement action and apply this code consistently across the community.

### 🤲 Reviews

If you are subject to an enforcement action and believe it was applied unfairly, you may reply to the notification you receive and ask a maintainer to review it.

---

## 🚦 No Retaliation

Do not retaliate against anyone who reports a concern in good faith. Retaliation is itself a violation of this code and will be treated as such.

Thank you for helping keep Better Bookmarks 2 a welcoming, productive place to build software together.
